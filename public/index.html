ABOUT OUR WEBSITE

A simpler way to explore restaurants.

Fork & Find is a restaurant directory designed to make browsing and comparing restaurants easier. Search by restaurant name or cuisine, explore restaurants by borough, and review inspection-score summaries using MongoDB aggregation.

Our goal is to organize restaurant information in one accessible place so visitors can discover dining options with less effort.
